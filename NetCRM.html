NetCRM

What problem does it solve? Support teams manage subscribers through WhatsApp and phone calls. NetCRM will give them a proper tool to track subscribers, plans, and support tickets without needing expensive enterprise software.

My approach Design the interface around real support workflows first, fast lookups and full subscriber history at a glance, before touching the data model.

The hardest thing I expect to learn Building for non-technical users. I know I'll be tempted to add flexibility and options, but the real challenge will be keeping things simple enough that anyone can pick it up without training.
