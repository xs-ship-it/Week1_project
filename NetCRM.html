Project

NetCRM

Web-based CRM for ISP subscriber management and support tickets.

Problem

Support teams manage subscribers through WhatsApp and phone calls. NetCRM gives them a proper tool to track subscribers, plans, and support tickets without needing expensive enterprise software.

Approach

Design the interface around real support workflows first — fast lookups and full subscriber history at a glance — before touching the data model.

Hardest challenge

Building for non-technical users. The real challenge will be keeping things simple enough that anyone can pick it up without training.
