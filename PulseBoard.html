PulseBoard

What problem does it solve? Providers with hundreds of active sessions have no live visibility into their network. PulseBoard will give them a real-time dashboard for active VPN/PPPoE sessions and bandwidth so they can catch problems before subscribers do.

My approach Start with a polling architecture to get something working, then move to WebSockets for streams where latency actually matters. Every metric on the dashboard has to earn its place.

The hardest thing I expect to learn Scaling real-time data. Pushing raw data to the frontend won't hold up under load, figuring out what to aggregate server-side versus what to send to the client will be the key challenge.
