Project

PulseBoard

Real-time dashboard for VPN/PPPoE session monitoring and bandwidth.

Problem

Providers with hundreds of active sessions have no live visibility into their network. PulseBoard gives them a real-time dashboard for active VPN/PPPoE sessions and bandwidth so they can catch problems before subscribers do.

Approach

Start with a polling architecture to get something working, then move to WebSockets for streams where latency actually matters. Every metric on the dashboard has to earn its place.

Hardest challenge

Scaling real-time data. Pushing raw data to the frontend will not hold up under load — figuring out what to aggregate server-side versus what to send to the client will be the key challenge.
